# Site Architecture

## Goal

The goal of the NOIR website is to help football communities discover,
design, and order custom sportswear while establishing NOIR as a trusted
custom sportswear brand from West Kalimantan.

The website is designed as a **brand-first experience**, not a
product-first or e-commerce-first website. The homepage should introduce
NOIR's identity and story before presenting products and conversion.

## Architecture Principles

### Brand-first Homepage

The homepage is designed to tell the NOIR story before introducing
products.

The intended progression is:

``` text
Identity
↓
Story
↓
Proof
↓
Community
↓
Lifestyle
↓
Products
↓
Process
↓
Conversion
```

### Product Timing

Products intentionally appear after the brand story, projects,
community, and lifestyle sections.

The goal is for products to feel like the natural expression of the NOIR
brand rather than the starting point of the website.

### Proof Over Generic Claims

NOIR does not need a separate generic "Why NOIR" section.

Quality, credibility, community, identity, and process should be
demonstrated throughout the website through real projects, real people,
photography, and the actual working process.

### Visual Hierarchy

Each homepage section should have a distinct visual role rather than
repeating the same card/grid pattern.

The homepage should feel editorial, premium, spacious, and image-led
while remaining clear and usable.
