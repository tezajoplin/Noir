# Brand DNA

## Purpose

NOIR exists because every football community deserves an identity they can proudly wear.

West Kalimantan has a deep-rooted football culture. New communities and teams are constantly formed, yet many still rely on generic jersey templates with only their team name added. A jersey should represent more than a name—it should represent who the team is.

NOIR exists to help football communities build an identity that truly reflects their character. Through thoughtful design, reliable craftsmanship, fast production, and competitive pricing, we make custom sportswear accessible without compromising quality.

## Target Market

### Primary Target

- Football communities in West Kalimantan.

### Secondary Target

- Local football teams.
- Football academies.
- Futsal teams.
- Schools, companies, and other sports communities.

## Value Proposition

### Quality

Premium materials, durable stitching, and long-lasting printing ensure every jersey is built to perform and last.

### Fast Turnaround

Efficient production and responsive communication help teams receive their jerseys on time.

### Competitive Pricing

High-quality custom sportswear at prices that can compete with manufacturers from Java.

### Wide Product Range

Beyond football jerseys, NOIR also offers custom apparel for basketball, badminton, futsal, and other sports.

## Brand Positioning

> We build team identities, not just jerseys.

NOIR exists to help sports communities express who they are through high-quality custom apparel. While many manufacturers simply produce jerseys, NOIR focuses on creating an identity that teams can proudly represent on and off the field.

## Brand Values

### Identity First

Every product should strengthen a team's identity, not just fulfill an order.

### Quality Matters

We never compromise on materials, craftsmanship, or finishing.

### Customer Partnership

We work together with customers to bring their vision to life.

### Continuous Improvement

We continuously improve our products, services, and processes based on feedback and innovation.
